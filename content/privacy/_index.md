Puzzlepia is a simple static website operated by BornstarSoft.

At this stage, Puzzlepia does not provide user accounts, a login system, a payment system, a ranking system, or a dynamic backend on this website.

We may receive basic technical information that browsers and hosting providers normally process when you visit a website, such as request time, page URL, device or browser information, and approximate region. This information is used to keep the website available, secure, and working properly.

Puzzlepia uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate site usage and page performance. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled with EU visitor data excluded. Puzzlepia does not use Google Analytics 4 on this website.

For convenience, Puzzlepia may store the most recently opened Daily Puzzle in your browser's local storage. This recent-page shortcut stays on your device, does not contain scores or puzzle progress, and can be cleared from the recent-puzzle panel.

Word Search also offers an optional **Remember my progress on this browser** setting, off by default. When enabled, it stores the latest set, found words (including words revealed with answer buttons), puzzle date, and save time in local storage. This progress is not sent to Puzzlepia or synced to other devices. Saves expire after 30 days without a new save and are removed when the Word Search page next reads them. Turn the setting off on that page to delete the save, or clear this site's data in your browser. Clearing the recent-page shortcut does not delete Word Search progress. Browser settings or private browsing may prevent saving or remove it earlier.

If you contact us by email, we may use the information you provide to respond to your message.

For privacy questions, contact [support@puzzlepia.com](mailto:support@puzzlepia.com).
