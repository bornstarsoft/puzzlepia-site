Sixteen number tiles, one target: **10**. Choose pairs anywhere on the board; they do not need to touch. Find all eight pairs to finish. Try today's layout or pick one of seven fixed boards, without a timer or score.

{{< daily-pair-board >}}

## How to play

1. Tap or click one tile, then a different tile whose number brings the total to 10. Keyboard players can use Tab and Enter or Space.
2. A valid pair stays marked with check marks. A different total gives feedback; your next tap begins a new pair.
3. Use **Clear selection** to change your choice. **Reset puzzle** restores every number in your current layout, even after completion.

The large numbers are the values to add. The small numbers in the corners identify positions. Two separate 5 tiles make a pair; tapping one tile twice does not.

## A different layout for your next break

The daily pick changes at **00:00 UTC** and repeats every seven days. These are curated practice boards, not newly generated daily puzzles. An open board stays unchanged, even across midnight; **Today's layout** opens the current pick.

You can choose any layout at any time, or use **Try next layout** after completing one. Switching layouts starts fresh. Progress is not saved when you leave this game.

To return to the same board on another day, use **Open a fresh Layout** above the tiles and bookmark that page. The link remembers the layout number, not your matches. The regular game address still opens today's pick.
