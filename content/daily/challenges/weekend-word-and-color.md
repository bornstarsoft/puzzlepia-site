Finish two tiny goals for a mixed weekend warmup.

## Puzzle prompt

First, find the word hidden from left to right:

`P L A Y M S`

Then count the pink tiles:

`Pink  Mint  Pink  Gold  Purple  Pink`

## Hint

The word uses the first four letters. Count only tiles labeled `Pink`.

## Answer

The hidden word is `PLAY`. There are three pink tiles.
