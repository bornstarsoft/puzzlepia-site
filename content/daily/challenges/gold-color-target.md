## Puzzle prompt

Count the gold tiles in this row:

`Gold  Mint  Pink  Gold  Purple  Gold  Mint`

## Hint

Read from left to right and count only the word `Gold`.

## Answer

There are three gold tiles, in positions 1, 4, and 6.
