Read a clue, think of a four-letter word, then find it on the board. Each word uses four neighboring letters in one straight line. Rows count down from the top; columns count across from the left.

## Round 1: What moves a kite?

**Clue:** Moving air that can lift a kite or shake leaves. What is the word? Find its starting and ending squares.

{{< word-search-example set="weather" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 hint" >}}
Look for a **W** on the bottom row. Check the letter directly above it before reading across.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 answer" >}}
The word is **WIND**. Read **column 5, from row 6 up to row 3**: W, I, N, D. Start on the bottom row and keep the column fixed as the row numbers decrease.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Keep Round 2 for next time](#round-2-ice-in-a-storm), or try a clue that runs in a different direction.

## Round 2: Ice in a storm

**Clue:** Small, hard pieces of ice that fall during some storms. The answer has four letters. Find the word on the [same board](#round-1-what-moves-a-kite), but do not assume it runs upward.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 hint" >}}
Look at the **H** in the top-left corner. Can you keep the row fixed and follow the next three letters?
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 answer" >}}
The word is **HAIL**. Read **row 1, from column 1 across to column 4**: H, A, I, L. The row stays fixed this time, while the column numbers increase.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
