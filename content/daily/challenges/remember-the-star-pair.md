## Puzzle prompt

Open the cards below. Notice the two **star** positions, then close the cards and name their small position numbers. Take as long as you like. This is a fixed practice exercise, not a timed test.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Look at the cards" >}}

![Positions 1 to 8: star, moon, sun, diamond, sun, diamond, star, moon.](/images/guides/memory-pairs-example.svg)

{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Hint" >}}

One star is at the start of the top row. The other is the third card in the bottom row. Count positions from left to right, starting at 1.

{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Answer and explanation" >}}

The stars are at **1 and 7**. Both show the same symbol, even though their position numbers differ. The other pairs are moons at 2 and 8, suns at 3 and 5, and diamonds at 4 and 6.
