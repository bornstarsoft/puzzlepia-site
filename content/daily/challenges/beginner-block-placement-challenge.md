## Plan your first three moves

Fit **Line 3, Square and Corner** on this empty 6x6 board without overlap. You do not need to fill the board or clear a line. Placing all three pieces completes the playable starter.

The pieces keep their orientation:

- **Line 3**: three cells across.
- **Square**: two rows of two cells.
- **Corner**: one cell on top, with two cells below it extending to the right.

Choose a starting cell for each piece. Count rows from the top and columns from the left. The starting cell is the piece's top-left occupied cell, not its center.

This is a reading diagram of the game's starting board, not a tappable game. There is more than one solution; plan yours before opening the example.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Keep the three-cell line in the top row. Leave two clear rows for the square and another two for the corner. An empty starting cell is not enough: every cell in the piece must fit.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show one solution" >}}
One valid plan is:

1. **Line 3: row 1, column 1.** It fills row 1, columns 1, 2 and 3.
2. **Square: row 3, column 1.** It fills rows 3 and 4, columns 1 and 2.
3. **Corner: row 5, column 5.** It fills row 5, column 5, then row 6, columns 5 and 6.

All **10 cells** are inside the board and none overlap. Empty cells are expected at the finish. This is one solution, not a claim that corners are always the best place for every piece.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Try your plan

Open [Daily Block Puzzle](/daily/block-puzzle/). Tap a named piece, then its starting cell. Try your own plan or the example above. A placement that does not fit leaves the board unchanged. Reset puzzle restores the empty board and all three pieces, including after completion. This starter has no score, line clearing or saved progress.
