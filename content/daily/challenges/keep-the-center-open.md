This challenge is about protecting the most flexible part of a block board.

## Puzzle prompt

You can place a three-block line along an open edge or across the middle of an empty 5x5 board.

Which placement usually keeps more options open for the next piece?

## Hint

The center can connect shapes arriving from several directions. An edge has fewer directions to protect.

## Answer

Place the line along the edge. This keeps the center open and leaves a larger connected area for future pieces.
