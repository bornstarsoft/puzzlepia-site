Each line shows a balanced scale. The items on the left of `=` weigh as much as the items on the right. A plain number stands for a weight in units: `8` means eight units.

Every shape weighs a whole number from **1 to 9 units**, and copies of the same shape weigh the same within a round. Start fresh for the second round.

## Round 1: Two matching stars

- `Star + Star = 8`
- `Moon + Star = 10`

How much does each shape weigh?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 hint" >}}
Start with the first scale. Split eight units into two equal weights.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 solution" >}}
**Star: 4 units. Moon: 6 units.**

Two stars make 8, so one star weighs 4. On the second scale, the moon must make up the 6 units needed to reach 10.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Keep Round 2 for next time](#round-2-remove-matching-shapes), or try it now.

## Round 2: Remove matching shapes

- `Star + Ring = Star + 3`
- `Moon + Ring = 8`
- `Moon + Star = 12`

Find all three weights. Can you solve the first scale without knowing what a star weighs?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 hint" >}}
Take one star off each side of the first scale. The same weight comes off both sides, so what remains must still balance.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 solution" >}}
**Star: 7 units. Moon: 5 units. Ring: 3 units.**

After removing the stars, the ring balances 3 units. The moon and ring total 8, so the moon weighs 5. The moon and star total 12, so the star weighs 7. Check the first scale: with a star on each side, both sides total 10.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
