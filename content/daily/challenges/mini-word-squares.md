Choose a word for each row. Column 1 must read the same as row 1, column 2 the same as row 2, and so on. Read rows from left to right and columns from top to bottom.

For example, write IN above NO. The first column reads IN and the second reads NO. That's a two-letter word square.

Solve on paper or in your head. Use each chosen word once as a row. Some words in each bank will be left over.

## Round 1: Complete the three-letter square

**Word bank:** `ARE, ARM, CAT, CUP, TEN, TOP`

The first row is `CAT`. Which two words go underneath, in order?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 hint" >}}
The first column also has to read CAT. That tells you what the next two rows start with. Then compare the last letter of row 2 to the middle letter of row 3.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 solution" >}}
From top to bottom: `CAT, ARE, TEN`.

Row 2 starts with A and row 3 starts with T. The last letter of ARE is E, which fits the middle of TEN. The M in ARM and the O in TOP have no match. Read the columns to check: they spell CAT, ARE, and TEN.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Keep Round 2 for next time](#round-2-choose-the-first-word), or try the larger square now.

## Round 2: Choose the first word

**Word bank:** `AREA, ARTS, BEAR, LAMB, LAMP, MEAL, MELT, PALS`

The top-left letter is `L`. Pick four different words to make a four-letter square. This time, even the first word is up to you.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 hint" >}}
Try both L-words as row 1. What does each choice require at the start of row 4? The second letter of row 4 must match the last letter of row 2.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 solution" >}}
From top to bottom: `LAMP, AREA, MEAL, PALS`.

- LAMB would put BEAR in row 4. Its second letter is E, but neither A-word ends in E. Use LAMP instead, with PALS in row 4.
- PALS has A as its second letter and L as its third. That forces row 2 to end in A and row 3 to end in L. Choose AREA and MEAL.
- Read the columns as a final check: LAMP, AREA, MEAL, and PALS.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
