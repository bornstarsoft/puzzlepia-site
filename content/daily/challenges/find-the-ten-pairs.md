## Puzzle prompt

Use every tile exactly once to make **four pairs that each add up to 10**. Match the large values; the small numbers identify positions. Partners can be anywhere on the board. You can solve this fixed exercise on paper or in your head.

{{< figure src="/images/guides/make-ten-example.svg" width="560" height="300" alt="Values at positions 1 to 8 are 2, 5, 9, 6, 4, 1, 5, 8." >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Hint" >}}

Start with the 2 at position 1. Ten minus two is eight: which position holds an 8? Remember that two different 5 tiles can also form a pair.

{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Answer and explanation" >}}

- Positions **1 and 8**: 2 + 8 = 10.
- Positions **2 and 7**: 5 + 5 = 10.
- Positions **3 and 6**: 9 + 1 = 10.
- Positions **4 and 5**: 6 + 4 = 10.

Every position appears once. You can choose the pairs in any order; there is no required path between tiles.

{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
