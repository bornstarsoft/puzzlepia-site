## Round 3: the same value, different tiles

Find the four **5 tiles at positions 1, 6, 12 and 15**. Use each of those tiles once to make two pairs that add up to ten. Can you find a different pairing without reusing a tile?

The large numbers are values; the small labels identify positions from left to right. Partners can be anywhere on the board. This is the actual fixed **Layout 4** from Make 10, with no timer or preferred pairing.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Start with the 5 at position 1. Any of the other three 5 tiles can be its partner. After choosing one, look at the two 5 tiles left over. Tapping position 1 twice does not count as two tiles.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the pairings and explanation" >}}
There are three ways to divide these four positions into two pairs:

- **1 + 6**, then **12 + 15**.
- **1 + 12**, then **6 + 15**.
- **1 + 15**, then **6 + 12**.

These are position labels, not values to add. Each pair uses two different **5** tiles, so its total is **10**. Reversing a pair or changing which pair you play first does not create a different grouping.

Whichever grouping you choose, finish the board with these six pairs of positions:

- **2 + 4** and **13 + 11**: each is 8 + 2.
- **3 + 7** and **14 + 16**: each is 4 + 6.
- **5 + 8**: 1 + 9.
- **9 + 10**: 3 + 7.

All sixteen tiles are used once. Correct pairs leave partners available; there is no path or adjacency restriction in this starter.
