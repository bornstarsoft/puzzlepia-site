## Alternating jumps

The sequence uses two different positive jumps, repeating them in the same order. What number goes in the blank?

`2, 4, 7, 9, ?, 14`

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the alternating jumps hint" >}}
Compare 2 to 4, then 4 to 7. The first of those jumps appears again from 7 to 9.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the alternating jumps answer" >}}
The missing number is **12**. The jumps are +2, +3, +2, +3, +2. Add 3 to 9, then check that adding 2 to 12 gives the final 14.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Two tracks in one line

This line alternates between two tracks. Positions 1, 3, 5 and 7 rise by the same amount each time. Positions 2, 4, 6 and 8 fall by the same amount each time. Fill the two blanks in their original order.

`1, 18, 4, 16, 7, ?, ?, 12`

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the two tracks hint" >}}
Separate the line into 1, 4, 7, ? and 18, 16, ?, 12. Compare terms within each track, not the neighbours in the original line.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the two tracks answer" >}}
The blanks are **14, then 10**. The first blank is position 6 on the falling track: 18, 16, 14, 12. The second is position 7 on the rising track: 1, 4, 7, 10. Put them back in the order of the blanks, not in numerical order.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
