## Round 3: finish in order

Imagine you have already found 1 through 12. Locate **13, 14, 15 and 16** on this board. Which positions would you tap to finish?

You are planning the last four moves, not choosing the largest visible number first. The large numbers are values; the small labels identify positions from left to right. This is a fixed reading exercise using actual **Layout 7** from Daily Number Puzzle.

{{< number-order-example layout="7" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Find 13 in the third row. Then scan for each next whole number, even when it sits above a value you just found. Board position does not decide numerical order.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the finish and explanation" >}}
Choose values **13, 14, 15, 16** at positions **10, 3, 12, 5**:

- **13**: third row, second column.
- **14**: top row, third column.
- **15**: third row, fourth column.
- **16**: second row, first column.

There is no requirement to connect these cells. If 16 catches your eye early, keep its location in mind but still choose 13, 14 and 15 first. In the game, the next-number message confirms what to find.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
