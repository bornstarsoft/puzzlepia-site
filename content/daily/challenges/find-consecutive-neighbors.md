Find two numbers that share an edge and differ by exactly **1**. Check across and down. Diagonal neighbors do not count. Each board has one answer.

## Round 1: Layout 3

{{< number-order-example layout="3" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 1 hint" >}}
Compare neighboring numbers in the first column. Some have only a small difference, but only one pair has a difference of 1.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 1 answer" >}}
**6 and 7** share an edge in the first column, at **positions 13 and 9**. From 6 to 7, move up one row. They are the only neighboring pair with a difference of 1 on this board.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Play Number Puzzle: Layout 3](/daily/number-puzzle/?layout=3), or [keep Round 2 for another break](#round-2-layout-6).

## Round 2: Layout 6

{{< number-order-example layout="6" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 2 hint" >}}
Check the end of the top row and the number just below it.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 2 answer" >}}
**10 and 11** share an edge in the last column, at **positions 4 and 8**. This time, the next number is one row down. No other horizontal or vertical neighbors differ by 1.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
