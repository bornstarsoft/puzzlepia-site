Can you fit **all three pieces into rows 1 and 2** of the Daily Block Puzzle board? There are six columns. The pieces do not rotate, overlap, or clear.

- **Line 3** fills three cells across.
- **Square** fills a 2x2 space.
- **Corner** fills one cell, the cell below it, and the one to its right on the bottom row.

You choose where each piece starts. Plan all three before you tap. The two-row limit is a challenge you set yourself; the game still allows placements farther down.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Both the Corner and Square need room on row 2. Try putting one of them at the far left. Then look for three spaces in a row for Line 3.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show one way to fit all three" >}}
Start **Corner at row 1, column 1**. It fills row 1 column 1, and row 2 columns 1 and 2. Put **Square at row 1, column 5**. That leaves **row 1, columns 2 through 4** for Line 3.

The pieces fill ten cells, but they do not need to fill the entire rectangle. Two cells in row 2 stay empty.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Play it, then swap the plan

[Play Daily Block Puzzle](/daily/block-puzzle/). Place the three pieces using only the top two rows. The game completes when all three fit. After a successful round, reset the board and try again with the Square on the far left.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the second solution" >}}
Start **Square at row 1, column 1**, **Corner at row 1, column 3**, and **Line 3 at row 1, column 4**. The Corner uses columns 3 and 4 on row 2. Line 3 runs across columns 4 through 6 on row 1, so they do not overlap.
