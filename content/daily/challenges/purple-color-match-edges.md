Find the **Purple** tiles on the outside edge of this board. The edge includes the top and bottom rows and the first and last columns. Count each corner only once. Then look inside the edge: **which purple positions would you miss if you stopped there?**

Read the color names as well as the fills. The small labels give each tile's position. This diagram is a fixed reading puzzle.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Once you finish the edge, scan only the middle 3x3 square. A purple tile can sit beside an edge tile without being on the edge itself.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the edge and inside positions" >}}
Five purple tiles are on the edge: **6, 11, 20, 24, 25**. The two still inside are **12 and 19**. Position 12 sits next to 11. Position 19 sits next to 20 and above 24. There are seven purple tiles in all.

Checking the edge first gives you a place to start. Checking the middle separately makes it less likely that you overlook a tile beside a group you already counted.
