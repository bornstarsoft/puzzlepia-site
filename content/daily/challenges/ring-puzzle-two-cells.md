Can you fit **five rings in two cells**? Start with **Mint large in cell 1** and **Gold medium in cell 2**, the first two cells in the top row. Now fit the three pink rings in these same cells.

Each cell can hold one small, one medium and one large ring. Rings of different sizes can share a cell. Colors do not affect what fits, and no rings clear in this starter.

{{< ring-practice-board id="two-cell-finish" >}}

**Which cell must take Pink large? Which cell must take Pink medium?** Decide where to put Pink small after those two.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Check the size that is already taken in each cell. Mint large and Pink large need the same position, even though they are different colors.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show both ways to finish" >}}
Put **Pink large in cell 2** because the large position in cell 1 is taken. Put **Pink medium in cell 1** because Gold medium is already in cell 2.

Neither cell has a small ring, so **Pink small fits in either cell**. The two large rings and the two medium rings need separate homes. Only the small ring can change places.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Try both in the game

[Play Daily Ring Puzzle](/daily/ring-puzzle/). It opens empty. Place Mint large and Gold medium as shown, then finish with Pink small in cell 1. Reset and finish with Pink small in cell 2 instead. The two-cell limit is a challenge you set yourself; the other cells still work normally.
