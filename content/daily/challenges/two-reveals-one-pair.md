## Keep one location in mind

Imagine your first two cards are **1: circle** and **2: star**. They do not match. On your next turn, you open **card 7**, which is a star. Which card should you choose next?

You can solve this from the three reveals alone. The optional board below shows all twelve faces from the game's Layout 2. Position numbers identify places, not matching values. This is a fixed, untimed practice exercise.

{{< memory-pairs-example layout="2" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
One card in your first turn already showed the symbol on card 7. Think of its location rather than opening an unknown card.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Answer and a second step" >}}
Choose **card 2**. Cards **7 and 2** both show stars. The earlier mismatch gave you a useful location even though it did not find a pair.

You also know that **card 1 is a circle**. Later, card **10** reveals the other circle, so **10 and 1** make another pair. You do not need to memorise the entire board at once.

The remaining pairs are **3 and 8** (triangles), **4 and 9** (suns), **5 and 12** (moons), and **6 and 11** (diamonds). Each card appears in exactly one pair.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
