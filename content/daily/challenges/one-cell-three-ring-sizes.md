One ring cell can hold several rings when their sizes are different.

## Puzzle prompt

A cell already contains a large pink ring and a small mint ring.

Which ring can still fit?

- another large ring
- a medium gold ring
- another small ring

## Hint

The starter rule allows one small, one medium, and one large ring in the same cell.

## Answer

The medium gold ring can fit because the medium position is open. Another large or small ring would duplicate a size already in the cell.
