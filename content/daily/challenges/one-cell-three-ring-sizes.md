## One full cell, one ring left

You have placed four of Daily Ring Puzzle's five tray rings:

- **Cell 1** holds Pink small, Pink medium and Pink large.
- **Cell 2** holds Mint large.
- **Cell 3** is empty.

Cells 1, 2 and 3 are the top row, from left to right. The other six cells are also empty, but this question compares only the three shown here.

{{< ring-practice-board id="starter-finish" >}}

The remaining ring is **Gold medium**. Which of the three shown cells can hold it? There may be more than one answer. Solid rings are occupied sizes; dashed outlines are empty. The names below each diagram describe its layers.

This is a fixed reading exercise. Rings do not move or clear, and matching colors are not required for placement.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Check the medium position in each cell. A cell does not have to be completely empty to accept the final ring.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show both valid choices" >}}
**Cells 2 and 3 both accept Gold medium.** Cell 2 has only its large position occupied; cell 3 has every size empty.

**Cell 1 cannot accept it.** Pink medium already occupies that size. Gold is a different color, but it still needs the same medium position.

Either valid choice places the fifth ring and completes this starter. Neither finish earns a better score: this game has no scoring or line clears. The unshown empty cells are also compatible; 2 and 3 are the answers within this diagram.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Compare the two finishes

Open [Daily Ring Puzzle](/daily/ring-puzzle/). From an empty board, put Pink large, Pink small and Pink medium in cell 1, then Mint large in cell 2. You now have the state shown above.

Select Gold medium and try cell 1: the board stays unchanged. Tap cell 2 instead to complete the game. Reset, rebuild the same four-ring setup, and put Gold medium in cell 3 to try the other finish. Reset restores every ring; it does not save either arrangement.
