Find two different **2x2 squares** on the same board. Each square uses two neighboring rows and two neighboring columns, with no gaps. Use the printed color names as well as the fills. The small labels tell you each tile's position.

## Round 1: Find one of each color

Which square contains **Pink, Mint, Gold and Purple**, with each color appearing once? Look for four tiles that form a square, not just four different colors anywhere on the board.

{{< color-match-example >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 hint" >}}
Look at positions 7 and 8. They sit side by side and have different colors. Check the two tiles directly below them.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 1 answer" >}}
The square uses **positions 7, 8, 12 and 13**. The top pair is Pink and Mint. Below them are Purple and Gold. This is the only 2x2 square with all four colors.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Play Color Match: Gold](/daily/color-match/?target=gold) and start with the Gold tile from your square. Then find the other five Gold tiles. The game itself does not require squares.

[Keep Round 2 for next time](#round-2-find-the-solid-square), or try the opposite rule now.

## Round 2: Find the solid square

On the [same board](#round-1-find-one-of-each-color), find the only **2x2 square where all four tiles share one color**. Which color is it? Two matching neighbors are a good start, but check both tiles in the next row.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 hint" >}}
Look for two matching tiles side by side near the bottom. Is there an identical pair in the row above or below?
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the Round 2 answer" >}}
The square is **Purple**, at **positions 19, 20, 24 and 25** in the bottom-right corner. Other pairs of neighboring tiles share a color, but none form a second four-tile square of one color.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Play Color Match: Purple](/daily/color-match/?target=purple). Clear the four tiles in your square, then find the three other Purple tiles. Both play links open a fresh board. Reset keeps the selected target and restores every tile.

For a different way to scan the same board, try [Find Purple Past the Edges](/daily/challenges/purple-color-match-edges/) or return to [Color Puzzle Strategy Basics](/blog/color-puzzle-strategy-basics/).
