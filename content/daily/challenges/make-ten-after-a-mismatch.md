## Find the next pair

In Make 10, choose two different tiles whose values add up to 10. They do not need to touch. Use the large numbers for the sum and the small position labels to find the tiles.

Imagine you have matched **position 1 (4)** with **position 4 (6)**. Then you try **position 6 (2)** and **position 9 (7)**. They add up to 9, so the game leaves that attempt highlighted.

**Can you match the 2 without losing your first pair?** Find its partner and decide which two positions to tap next. The diagram shows the original board so the position labels stay easy to check.

{{< number-pairs-example layout="6" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Subtract 2 from 10. Look for the result in the same row as position 6. The next tap starts a fresh pair.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the recovery and answer" >}}
**Tap position 8 (8), then position 6 (2).** Tapping the 8 clears the previous attempt and makes the 8 the first tile of a new pair. Tapping the 2 completes that pair. You can also select the 2 first, then the 8.

The matched 4 and 6 stay matched throughout. You now have two pairs out of eight. You can use **Clear selection** before selecting the new pair, but you do not need **Reset puzzle**, which would remove all your matches.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
