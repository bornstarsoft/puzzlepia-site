Warm up with four short fruit words.

## Puzzle prompt

Find one fruit word in each row:

```text
P E A R
L I M E
P L U M
K I W I
```

The target words are `PEAR`, `LIME`, `PLUM`, and `KIWI`.

## Hint

Read each row from left to right.

## Answer

The rows spell `PEAR`, `LIME`, `PLUM`, and `KIWI` in order from top to bottom.
