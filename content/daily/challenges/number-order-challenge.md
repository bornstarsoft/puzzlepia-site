## One wrong tap, no restart needed

Imagine you have correctly tapped **1 through 5**, then tapped **7** too soon. The game says **"Look for 6 first."** Which number should you tap next, and where is it?

Use the large values, not the small position labels. This reading diagram shows the full, unmarked **Layout 4** from Daily Number Puzzle; imagine the first five values are already done. Positions count from left to right, top to bottom.

{{< number-order-example layout="4" >}}

Find **6, 7 and 8** in that order. Do you need to reset, or can you keep going from 6?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
The status names the next required value. Look for 6 in the bottom row. The tile at position 6 is a different number; its small label is only an address.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the recovery route" >}}
Tap **6 at position 16**, the bottom-right cell. Then tap **7 at position 3** and **8 at position 14**. The positions are **16, 3, 14**.

Position 6 contains the value **5**, which is already complete in this example. You are following number values, not visiting positions in order.

**No reset is needed.** The early tap on 7 does not complete it, undo 1 through 5, or advance the target. After 6, the same 7 tile becomes the correct next choice.

Reaching 8 finishes this short question, not the whole game. Continue through **9 to 16** for the completion message. This puzzle has no score or timer.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Try it on the same board

[Play Number Puzzle: Layout 4](/daily/number-puzzle/?layout=4). The link opens a fresh full board, not the imagined halfway state.

1. Tap 1 through 5. Their positions are **12, 2, 8, 9, 6**.
2. Tap 7 once. Check that the message asks for 6 and your first five tiles stay complete.
3. Tap 6, then 7 and 8. Keep going in order through 16.
4. Use Reset puzzle to replay. It restores every tile on Layout 4, including after completion.

For a later break, bookmark this question or the Layout 4 link and try the board without the deliberate wrong tap. The arrangement stays the same; the link does not save your completed tiles. If a tap goes wrong, check the target and continue.
