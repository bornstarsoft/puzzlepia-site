## Can three rings share one cell?

In Daily Ring Puzzle, place **Pink large** and **Pink small** in the top-left cell, **cell 1**. The diagram shows that state. A solid ring occupies a size; a dashed outline is empty. The labels give the same information without relying on color alone.

{{< ring-practice-board id="starter-nest" >}}

Which of these remaining tray rings can join cell 1?

- **Pink medium**.
- **Mint large**.

Choose by size, then explain why the other ring cannot fit. This is a fixed reading diagram, not a tappable board. Existing rings stay in place and nothing clears.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Read the small, medium and large positions separately. The two pink rings have different sizes. A different color does not create another large position.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the answer and explanation" >}}
**Pink medium fits in cell 1.** Its medium position is empty, so all three pink rings can share the cell.

**Mint large does not fit in cell 1.** Pink large already occupies that size. Mint large can use another cell with an empty large position.

Three pink rings do not trigger a clear in this starter. They remain visible. You have placed **3 of 5 rings**, so the game is not complete yet.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Try the same moves

Open [Daily Ring Puzzle](/daily/ring-puzzle/) and use Reset puzzle if needed. Cells count from left to right, top to bottom; cell 1 is the top-left, cell 2 the top-middle.

1. Select Pink large, then tap cell 1.
2. Select Pink small, then tap cell 1.
3. Select Mint large and try cell 1. The game rejects it without changing the board.
4. Select Pink medium, then tap cell 1. All three pink rings remain nested there.
5. Place Mint large and Gold medium in cell 2 to finish all five rings.

Reset restores the empty board and all five tray rings, even after completion. There is no line clearing, score or saved progress in this starter.
