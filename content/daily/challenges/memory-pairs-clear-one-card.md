You are playing Memory Pairs on Layout 7. The small numbers are card positions. Match the symbols, not those numbers.

1. Turn over **cards 1 and 10**. Both are stars, so that pair stays face up.
2. Turn over **card 3: diamond**, then **card 5: moon**. They do not match.
3. Next, open **card 8: diamond**. This turns the two mismatched cards face down and starts a fresh pair. You meant to look for moons instead.

**How can you turn card 8 face down without losing your stars?** After you do that, **card 12 reveals a moon**. Which earlier card should you choose to complete that pair?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
One control clears only the current selection. Keep the location where you saw the moon during the mismatch.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the recovery and answer" >}}
Choose **Clear selection**. Card 8 turns face down, but the stars at **1 and 10 stay matched**. Reset puzzle would remove that match as well.

Turn over **12**, then **5**. Both show moons, so you now have two pairs. You could also match the diamonds at 8 and 3 before looking for moons; clearing the selection is useful when you want to change your plan.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
