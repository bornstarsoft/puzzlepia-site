## Find one backward word

Find **KIWI** in this fixed practice board. The four letters are neighbours in a straight line, reading from right to left. Rows are numbered from the top; columns are numbered from the left.

{{< word-search-example set="fruit" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Look at the right-hand end of row 6. Start with K, then move left without leaving that row.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Answer and explanation" >}}
Start at **row 6, column 6** and move left through columns **6, 5, 4, 3**: K, I, W, I. The two I letters have W between them. Do not stop at the first I.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
