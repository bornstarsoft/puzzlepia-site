**Rule:** continue the shortest color block that repeats without changes. Each example shows at least two complete repeats. Fill steps **9 through 12**. Read left to right, then continue on the next row; a new row does not restart the pattern.

Color names carry the same information as the fills. These are fixed reading puzzles, not tappable game boards. The questions stay the same when you return, and no progress is saved.

## Round 1: three colors

{{< color-pattern-example id="three-colors" >}}

Which four colors continue the sequence? Check step 8 before choosing step 9.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 1 hint" >}}
Read the first three steps as one block. The block continues across the row boundary.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 1 answer" >}}
Steps 9 through 12 are **Gold, Pink, Mint, Gold**. The repeating block is **Pink, Mint, Gold**.

Step 8 is Mint, so Gold finishes that repeat. Pink starts the next block at step 10, not at the start of a new table row.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Round 2: colors in pairs

{{< color-pattern-example id="color-pairs" >}}

The colors are familiar, but the spacing has changed. What fills the four empty steps?

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 2 hint" >}}
Count how many times each color appears before it changes. One Mint followed by one Gold is not the rule here.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 2 answer" >}}
Steps 9 through 12 are **Mint, Mint, Gold, Gold**. The shortest repeating block is **Mint, Mint, Gold, Gold**.

There are two Mint steps, then two Gold steps. A two-step Mint/Gold alternation would disagree with steps 1 and 2.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

[Keep Round 3 for next time](#round-3-pink-returns), or try it now.

## Round 3: pink returns

{{< color-pattern-example id="returning-pink" >}}

Pink appears often. Does that make the whole pattern a two-color alternation? Fill all four steps before checking.

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 3 hint" >}}
Compare the colors immediately after the first Pink and the second Pink. They are not the same.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show Round 3 answer" >}}
Steps 9 through 12 are **Pink, Mint, Pink, Gold**. The shortest repeating block is **Pink, Mint, Pink, Gold**.

Pink occurs twice within the block. Looking only at Pink misses the difference between Mint at step 2 and Gold at step 4. Check a whole block before deciding it repeats.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
