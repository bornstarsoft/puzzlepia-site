Look for the smallest group that repeats.

## Puzzle prompt

Which color comes next?

`Pink  Mint  Gold  Pink  Mint  Gold  Pink  Mint  ?`

## Hint

The pattern repeats every three colors.

## Answer

`Gold` comes next. The repeating group is `Pink, Mint, Gold`.
