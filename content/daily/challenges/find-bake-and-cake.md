Find **BAKE** and **CAKE** on the Fresh baking board. Both end in **A K E**, but each word takes its own straight path. Which word is higher on the board? Try to find both before opening the answer.

{{< word-search-example set="baking" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Start with B, then C. From each one, check whether the next three letters in a straight line are A, K, E. Spotting A K E is not enough: the starting letter tells you which word you found.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show both word paths" >}}
**BAKE** is higher: **row 1, columns 1 to 4**, from left to right. **CAKE** also reads left to right, in **row 3, columns 1 to 4**. The two endings look the same, so check B or C before selecting an endpoint.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
