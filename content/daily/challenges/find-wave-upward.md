Find **WAVE** on the By the coast practice board. Start with W and check its neighbours. The four letters must follow one straight line without skipping a square. Which row holds the W, and which row holds the E at the other end?

{{< word-search-example set="coast" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Find the W near the bottom edge. Look above it for A. The letter immediately to its right is not A.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the WAVE path" >}}
**Column 1, from row 6 up to row 3** spells **W A V E**. The row numbers are **6, 5, 4, 3**; the column stays the same. The row beginning at W reads W N S Q across its first four cells, so that direction does not work.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Play the same board

[Play the By the coast set](/daily/word-search/?set=coast). Tap the W and then the E at the other end, or drag along the path. WAVE is one of four words. For another upward route, try SAND or TIDE. SURF runs across instead.

The set link opens the same board on another visit. Finding WAVE finishes this reading question; find all four words in the game to complete the board.
