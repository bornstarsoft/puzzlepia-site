## One word across

Find **STAR** from left to right on the First steps board. Give its row and the columns where it starts and ends. A nearby S and R are not enough: check that T and A fit between them in order.

{{< word-search-example set="starter" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Begin with the top row. Follow each letter immediately to the right of the S; do not skip a square or change rows.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show the answer and why it works" >}}
**Row 1, columns 1 to 4** spells **S T A R**. The row stays the same while the column increases by one each time.

The final row ends T A R, but there is no S directly before that sequence. A partial ending does not make a full match.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

## Try it on the same board

[Play the First steps Word Search set](/daily/word-search/?set=starter). Tap STAR's first and last letters, or drag along its line. The word list should mark STAR as found.

This reading question ends after one word. The playable game completes after you find **STAR, MOON, MINT and PLAY**. Reset clears all four words. The set link opens this fixed board, not the rotating daily pick.
