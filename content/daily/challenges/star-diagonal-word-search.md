## Follow a diagonal

Find **STAR** in this fixed Night Sky board. Each letter is one row down and one column to the right of the previous letter. Keep the line straight; do not turn a corner.

{{< word-search-example set="night-sky" >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Show a small hint" >}}
Use the S in the top-left corner. Its next letter, T, is diagonally below it to the right.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}

{{< puzzle-reveal title="Answer and explanation" >}}
The cells are **row 1, column 1** (S), **row 2, column 2** (T), **row 3, column 3** (A), and **row 4, column 4** (R). Both coordinates increase by one at every step, so all four letters share one diagonal.
{{< /puzzle-reveal >}}
