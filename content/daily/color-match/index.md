Choose a target color and find every matching tile on a fixed 5 by 5 board. Start with pink, or try mint, gold or purple for another short round. Color names appear on each tile, so you do not need to rely on color alone.

## How to play

Tap tiles with the target's name, in any order. They do not need to touch, and there are no line clears or falling tiles. Pink, mint and gold each have six targets; purple has seven. The progress message shows how many you have cleared.

A wrong-color tap gives feedback without removing a tile. When every target is cleared, the round is complete. **Reset puzzle** restores all 25 tiles and keeps your selected target, before or after completion.

## Another color for your next break

After completion, **Try next color** opens a fresh round on the same board. You can also use **Target color** at any time; switching restores all tiles.

Use **Open a fresh round** above the board to bookmark a particular target. That link starts again from zero, and the ordinary page address starts with pink. These are fixed practice rounds, not newly generated daily puzzles. There is no timer, score, streak or saved progress.
