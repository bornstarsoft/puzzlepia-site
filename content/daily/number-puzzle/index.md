Find the numbers from 1 to 16 in order. The progress message keeps the next target clear while you scan the grid. Numbers can be anywhere; you do not need to follow a connecting path.

## How to play

Tap the numbers in ascending order. A correct tap locks and marks the tile; an incorrect tap gives gentle feedback and lets you continue.

## Another layout for your next break

Choose from **seven fixed layouts**, or play today's pick. It changes at **00:00 UTC** and repeats weekly; these are curated boards, not newly generated puzzles. After finishing, **Try next layout** opens another board.

**Reset puzzle** restores your current layout, even after completion or midnight. Switching layouts starts fresh. An open board never changes on its own.

Use **Open a fresh Layout** near the board to bookmark the same arrangement for later. That link opens the chosen layout on any date; the regular game address opens today's pick. Neither saves your progress. There is no timer, score or streak.
